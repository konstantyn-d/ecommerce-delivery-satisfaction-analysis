# Sampling report

Generated by `notebooks/05_review_sample.py`. Do not edit by hand: re-run the script instead.

## Method

- **Frame:** reviews with a score of 1 or 2 and a written comment, on orders purchased from 2017-01-01 to 2018-08-31, one row per `review_id`: **10,636 reviews**.
- **Sample size:** 300 reviews.
- **Design:** stratified random sample with proportional allocation. Strata = delivery group of the order. Because each group has the same share in the sample as in the frame, the overall results need no weighting.
- **Randomisation:** reviews are sorted by `md5(review_id || '20261002')` inside each group and the first *n* are taken. The sample is therefore identical on every run and on every machine.
- **Blind coding:** the coding sheet contains only the score and the text. The delivery group is kept in `sample_key.csv` and joined after coding.
- **Pilot set:** the next 15 reviews of each group (60 in total), used to develop the codebook. None of them is in the sample.
- **Re-coding set:** 30 of the 300 sampled reviews, selected by hash, to measure whether the coder is consistent with himself.

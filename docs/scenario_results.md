## 5. Reference point

If every late order in the country were on time and the full observed difference were real, 3,391 bad reviews would be avoided (23.6% of all bad reviews) and the share of 1-2 star reviews would be 11.18%. This is the ceiling for any action on late deliveries alone.

## 6. Anchor for the case factors

The low, base and high factors are assumptions. One data point helps to place them: an order that stops being late does not necessarily become a fast order. On-time orders that took 22 days or more (5,965 reviewed orders) have a 1-2 star share of 15.8%, against 9.2% for all on-time orders and 62.4% for late orders. If former late orders were reviewed like these slow on-time orders, 88% of the full gap would be realised.
